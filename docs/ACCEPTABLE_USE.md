# Acceptable Use & Legal Notice

`monitor-com` is a tool for **lawful, transparent workplace monitoring**. Before
you deploy it, read this. It is not legal advice — consult counsel for your
jurisdiction — but it describes the only uses this project supports.

## Supported use

You may use `monitor-com` only when **all** of the following are true:

1. **Ownership / authority.** You own or lawfully administer the device, or you
   have explicit written authorization from the owner to monitor it.
2. **Notice.** The people using the device have been clearly informed, in
   advance, that the device is monitored and what is collected (screenshots,
   visited URLs, keystroke activity counts).
3. **Consent / lawful basis.** You have a lawful basis to monitor — in many
   jurisdictions this means informed consent, an employment agreement and/or an
   acceptable-use policy the user accepted. Some jurisdictions require explicit,
   separate consent for monitoring; some restrict or prohibit it entirely.
4. **Proportionality & purpose limitation.** You collect only what you need for
   a legitimate business purpose, retain it only as long as needed, and secure
   it appropriately.
5. **Compliance.** Your use complies with all applicable laws and regulations —
   e.g., GDPR and national labour/works-council rules in the EU/UK, ECPA and
   state wiretap/consent laws in the US (several require all-party consent),
   and sector rules that may apply.

## Prohibited use

This project does **not** support, and you must not use it for:

- Installing on devices you do not own or administer.
- **Covert monitoring** of people who have not been notified — including
  spouses, partners, family members, or any individual (this is "stalkerware"
  and is illegal in most jurisdictions).
- Capturing the **content** of keystrokes, credentials, or private
  communications. (The agent is designed not to do this; do not modify it to.)
- Evading detection, disabling security software, or defeating the device
  user's ability to see that monitoring is running.
- Any surveillance that is unlawful where the device or the user is located.

## How the software helps you stay compliant

- It requires a recorded **consent acknowledgement** before collecting.
- It shows a **tray icon and notice** so users can see it is running.
- It runs as a normal, visible process — no hiding.
- It lets you **disable** any collector and set **retention** limits.

If your intended use conflicts with this policy, stop and seek another approach.
